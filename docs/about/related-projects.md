# Related projects

These projects also detect and locate earthquakes by stacking and migrating seismic energy in 3D.

## Lassie v1

The predecessor of Qseek. Qseek uses the same stacking and migration functions.

[Lassie v1 on Pyrocko Git](https://git.pyrocko.org/pyrocko/lassie)

## QuakeMigrate

Migrates and stacks onset functions across a network to detect earthquakes, and produces catalogs with locations, phase picks, local magnitudes and location uncertainties. The STA/LTA image function of Qseek is adapted from QuakeMigrate.

[QuakeMigrate on GitHub](https://github.com/QuakeMigrate/QuakeMigrate)

## BPMF

Combines backprojection and matched filtering (BPMF) for earthquake detection and location, with automatic picking and relocation.

[BPMF on GitHub](https://github.com/ebeauce/Seismic_BPMF)

## LOKI

Detects and locates earthquakes by stacking waveform coherence along travel times (LOcation of seismic events through traveltime staKIng).

[LOKI on GitHub](https://github.com/wulwife/LOKI)

## MALMI

A variant of LOKI that stacks phase probabilities from machine learning pickers of SeisBench (MAchine Learning aided earthquake MIgration location).

[MALMI on GitHub](https://github.com/speedshi/MALMI)
