# Showcase

Qseek has been used to build earthquake catalogs in tectonic, volcanic and induced settings. The case studies of Reykjanes, the Eifel and Utah FORGE are published in the [Qseek paper](https://doi.org/10.26443/seismica.v4i1.1283) (Isken et al., 2025); Campi Flegrei is the [quick start](../getting-started/quick-start.md) of this documentation.

!!! tip "Using Qseek in your research?"
    We would like to show your results here. [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/pyrocko/qseek/issues) with a figure, a short description and the reference.

## Reykjanes Peninsula, Iceland

![Earthquakes detected by Qseek on the Reykjanes Peninsula](../images/reykjanes-demo.webp)
/// caption
More than 30 000 earthquakes detected during the 2020 unrest on the Reykjanes Peninsula, located with source-specific station corrections (SSST). Stations of the networks 7E, 9H, SI and ZB.
///

The seismicity of the 2020 unrest on the Reykjanes Peninsula outlines a dike intrusion, which preceded the 2021 Fagradalsfjall eruption. The map view and the depth section show the detections, scaled by their moment magnitude.

## Campi Flegrei, Italy

![Detections at Campi Flegrei in the web UI](../images/webui-overview.webp)
/// caption
The detections of 20 May 2024 at Campi Flegrei in the Qseek web UI, colored by depth and scaled by magnitude.
///

On 20 May 2024, a volcano-tectonic swarm with an Md 4.4 earthquake struck the Campi Flegrei caldera. With 18 stations of the INGV network, Qseek detects 732 events on that day, including all 45 earthquakes of the INGV catalog. The [quick start](../getting-started/quick-start.md) reproduces this search step by step.
